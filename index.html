Int. The Studio — Day

REEL 02 / THE DESIGNER

Before the interface, there's an argument nobody's won yet.

I trained as a graphic designer before UX had its own department. Five years later I still start every project the same way — not with a screen, but with a question: what is this business actually asking the user to believe, and does the interface make that easy, or a lie?

My work lives in the space between research and code: discovery, user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and the design-system plumbing that lets a team ship the fifth screen as fast as the first. I don't hand off and disappear — I stay for the build, the QA pass, and the version that comes after the users tell us the truth.

Ext. Three Cities — Various

REEL 04 / THE CASE FILES

Three problems. Three systems built to solve them.

Each file below ran the same production schedule — discovery, architecture, prototype, usability test, visual system, handoff — graded differently because the people inside them wanted different things.

CASE FILE — 01 / FINTECH

5-WEEK PRODUCTION

Flow

AI-assisted personal budgeting, for people the banking apps forgot.

Three people, one shared fear: not knowing where the money went until it was already gone.

Anna, 28 · Marketing Specialist

Lives paycheck to paycheck — needs clarity and simple savings goals.

Marek, 34 · Freelancer

Irregular income — needs cashflow forecasting and a tax bucket.

Sara, 22 · Student

Micro-budgets — wants a safe-to-spend number she can trust.

PRODUCTION SCHEDULE

Wk 1–2 Research (12 interviews · 2-week diary study · 150-respondent survey) → Wk 3 Information architecture & low-fi flows → Wk 4 Mid-fi prototype & usability test (n=6) → Wk 5 Visual system, design tokens & handoff.

NORTH STAR: WEEKLY ACTIVE PLANNERS DAY-1 ACTIVATION ≥35% WEEK-4 RETENTION ≥25% SAVINGS RATE +5%/3MO NPS ≥45

Financial design is trust wearing an interface. Transparency about data — not more features — was what got people to connect their first account.

CASE FILE — 02 / HEALTH & HABITS

4-WEEK PRODUCTION

HabitNest+

Fitness, food, and sleep — one small plan a day instead of one big overhaul.

Habits don't break from a lack of willpower. They break from friction, and from streaks that shame instead of invite.

Lana, 40 · Busy Parent

Wants a clear daily plan, not homework.

Olek, 29 · Beginner

Needs visible streaks to stay in it.

Ada, 33 · Runner

Wants structured training and recovery data.

PRODUCTION SCHEDULE

Wk 1 Research synthesis, personas, problem framing → Wk 2 IA, flows, low-fi wireframes, copy v1 → Wk 3 Mid-fi prototype & usability test (n=6) → Wk 4 Visual design, micro-copy & handoff.

NORTH STAR: WEEKLY ADHERENT USERS (4/7 DAYS) ONBOARDING ≥70% 8-WEEK RETENTION ≥30% DAILY STEPS +20% SLEEP SCORE +10%

“Streak paused, not lost” outperformed every progress bar we tested. Designing for the moment someone falls off mattered more than designing for the moment they start.

CASE FILE — 03 / SMART CITY & TRAVEL

4-WEEK PRODUCTION

CitySense

A map, an AR layer, and a sense of place — for travelers drowning in options.

Travelers don't want more listings. They want the right five, and confidence the map still works when the signal doesn't.

Irina, 26 · Weekend Traveler

Wants photogenic, walkable finds.

Piotr, 37 · Business Traveler

Needs late-night food and offline maps.

Nadia, 31 · Planner

Wants a real itinerary with real time budgets.

PRODUCTION SCHEDULE

Wk 1 Research, interest taxonomy, map pins → Wk 2 IA, low-fi map & AR wireframes → Wk 3 Mid-fi prototype & field test (6 users) → Wk 4 Visual design, motion & handoff (Figma + Lottie).

NORTH STAR: ITINERARY COMPLETION RATE PLACE SAVE RATE ≥40% SHARE RATE ≥15% AR USAGE ≥30% OF SESSIONS

Context beat completeness. Mood- and weather-aware suggestions earned more trust than a longer, “more accurate” list ever did.

REEL 05 / THE METHOD

Same five acts, every production.

  1. 01

    Discovery & Research — interviews, diary studies, surveys, competitive scans.

  2. 02

    Information Architecture & Flows — the map before the screens.

  3. 03

    Prototype & Usability Testing — low-fi to mid-fi, tested with real users before a pixel is precious.

  4. 04

    Visual System & Design Tokens — the part that scales past screen one.

  5. 05

    Developer Handoff & Validation — specs, states, edge cases, and staying in the room for the build.

I don't leave when the file is exported. I stay for the QA pass, the edge case nobody scoped, and the version two that real usage always demands.
